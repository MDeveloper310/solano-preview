Summary

Scroll California collects information you give us (account, reviews, forms), information about how you use the site (usage analytics), and, only if you choose, your location. We may sell personal information to, or share it with, partners and advertisers, including for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. You can opt out at any time with the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link, which is also in every page footer. We also honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt-out.

We don't collect Social Security, driver's license, financial account, health, biometric or similar sensitive information. Precise geolocation is the only sensitive category, it's optional, and we use it only to give you nearby results.

Information we collect

  • Account: email, a hashed password or sign-in links, display name, sign-in times, and (optional, private) a home city or ZIP and your Do Not Sell or Share choice.
  • Reviews & activity: ratings, review text, photo links, helpful votes, reports, likes, saves, spot-of-the-week votes and deal claims.
  • Forms (apply, claim, creator submission, quote, hire-a-creator, advertise, referral, contact): name, email, phone, ZIP, business details, social handles, video links and your message.
  • Usage analytics: first-party events (page view, video play, like, save, share, call, directions, website, quote and claim clicks) with the page or item and time. Our host (GitHub Pages) may log IP address and browser.
  • Location: see below.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, you're sent to beehiiv (reelcalifornia.beehiiv.com) with your email and the part of the site you came from.

Location (“Show spots near me”)

Location is off until you ask for it. We never look up your location from your IP address. When you tap Show spots near me, we first show a short notice, then your browser asks for permission. If you allow it, your precise location is used on your device to sort spots, videos and news by distance. It isn't sent to us or stored by us. If you decline, you can type a city or ZIP instead. If you're signed in, you can save a home city or ZIP to your private profile so nearby results work on any device. You can change or delete it on your Account page.

Trip planner (/trip/)

When you plan a route, your browser sends the start and end you type to OpenStreetMap Nominatim (to find the places) and the coordinates to the OSRM public routing server (router.project-osrm.org) to draw the route. Map images come from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Those services see your IP address under their own policies. We don't store route searches. If you're signed in and tap Save trip, we store the start and end names, your chosen needs and distance (not coordinates) in your account until you delete them. If you use “My location” as the start, your precise location goes from your browser to OSRM only and is never sent to us.

Profiles, playlists & privacy settings

Your profile (display name, handle, bio, initials avatar, follower counts) is public. Your activity wall shows your likes, reposts and reviews. You can make your profile private, hide likes or hide reposts, and set each playlist to public, unlisted (anyone with the link) or private. These settings are enforced in our database, not just hidden on screen. Reviews stay public on business pages. Watch later is always private. Without an account, saves and playlists stay on your device until you sign in, when they're copied to your account.

Stored on your device

The site uses your browser's localStorage for favorites, collections, “Been here”, recently viewed, likes, your last chosen city, your Do Not Sell or Share choice, and copies of forms that couldn't be sent. A service worker caches public pages for speed and offline use. Clear it anytime by clearing site data.

How we use it

To run the site and your account, publish and moderate reviews, answer requests, rank Trending and Viral content, personalize the feed, measure and sell advertising, prevent fraud and spam, and meet legal obligations. Hire-a-creator requests may go to our video partner, Crenshaw Visuals, and quote requests to the business you asked about.

Selling and sharing

We may sell or share (for cross-context behavioral advertising) identifiers, usage analytics, approximate location (home city or ZIP), commercial information and inferences to: advertisers and ad networks, data and analytics partners, and businesses and marketing partners listed on or advertising with Scroll California. We do not sell or share precise geolocation, and we don't sell or share personal information of consumers we know are under 16. In the past 12 months we have not sold personal information; this policy tells you before we start. If you opt out, we stop selling and sharing your information from that point on.

Your right to opt out (Do Not Sell or Share)

Use Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. No account needed. The choice is saved in your browser, and if you're signed in it's also saved to your account so it follows you. Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out for that browser (and your account, if you're signed in) without you doing anything else. You can also opt out through the contact form.

Service providers & third parties

  • GitHub Pages: hosting.
  • Supabase: accounts, reviews, private profile fields, forms and analytics events.
  • FormSubmit: form delivery to our inbox.
  • beehiiv: newsletter.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the OSRM demo server: address lookup and routing on the trip planner, called by your browser.
  • OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet (unpkg.com): on the map page, your browser requests map images and code directly from them.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode), TikTok and Instagram: video players load from those companies only when you scroll to or tap a video.

Cookies, embeds & similar technologies

We don't set advertising cookies ourselves today. We use localStorage and a service-worker cache as described above. Embedded players from YouTube, TikTok and Instagram may set cookies and collect data under those companies' policies once they load. The feed shows a lightweight preview first and loads a player only when you reach that video. If we add advertising or analytics tags, they will respect your opt-out and GPC, and we'll update this policy first.

Retention

See the table above. In general we keep account data while your account is open, form submissions and raw analytics events up to 24 months, invoices up to 7 years, and newsletter data until you unsubscribe. Precise location isn't retained.

Your California privacy rights

California residents have the right to: know and access the personal information we collect, use, disclose, sell or share; delete it; correct it; opt out of sale or sharing; limit the use of sensitive personal information (we use precise location only to give you nearby results); and not be discriminated against for using these rights. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We'll verify your request by email and respond within 45 days (we may extend that once by 45 days with notice). An authorized agent can submit a request with proof of authorization. Opt-out requests don't need verification.

Children

The site isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect, sell or share their personal information.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the “last updated” date. If we make material changes to how we sell or share data, we'll show a notice on the site first.
