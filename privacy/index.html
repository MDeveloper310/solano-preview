Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026. Plain-language draft: to be reviewed by a lawyer.

The short version

  • No tracking cookies and no ad networks. You only need an account if you upload a video.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information.
  • Our videos are hosted on this site, so watching them doesn't send your data to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.

What we collect

When you send a form

When you use a form (submit a spot, a sponsor inquiry or the newsletter), we receive what you type, like your name, email and message. Forms are delivered to our inbox by an email-forwarding service (FormSubmit). We use these details only to reply, review your submission or send the newsletter you asked for.

When you upload a video

To upload, you sign in with Google. We receive your email address and a user ID from Google (not your password or contacts). We store your video, caption, the place and city you picked, the time, the confirmations you ticked, and your display name if you gave one. Our database and file storage are run by Supabase on our behalf.

Before a video is published we re-encode it, which removes hidden metadata such as GPS location and the phone model. Published videos show only the display name you chose (or "a visitor"), never your email. The original upload is deleted from storage after publishing or rejection; rejected video records are kept up to 90 days for safety review.

Reports and claims

If you report a video or claim a business, we store what you type and, if you give it, your email, so we can follow up. To limit abuse we store a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address with reports and claims, not the address itself.

Near me

If you tap Near me on the search page, your browser asks for permission and your location is used only inside that page to sort results. It isn't sent to us or stored.

Site measurement

We may count visits, video views and clicks on sponsored placements so we can report totals to sponsors. If we do, we record the page, the type of event, the referring website and the time. We don't use cookies or device IDs for this, and we don't try to identify you. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we don't record anything.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We don't sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act. We honor Global Privacy Control signals automatically. You don't have to do anything.

Your choices

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act you can ask to know, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, including your account and uploads, and we won't treat you differently for asking. You can withdraw a pending upload yourself on the upload page. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. DM @crenshawvisuals or use the contact form and write "privacy request".

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Uploading requires being 18 or older.
