Privacy Policy

Last updated Oct 30, 2026

⚠️ Template. Have a lawyer review before launch.

Summary

We collect only what you give us through our forms and newsletter, plus basic technical data. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

Information we collect

  • Forms (apply, claim, creator submission, review, quote, hire-a-creator, advertise, referral, contact): name, email, business details, social handles, reel links and any message you write.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, you're sent to our newsletter provider, beehiiv (reelcalifornia.beehiiv.com), with your email filled in, along with which part of the site you came from. beehiiv stores your subscription under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical data: our host (GitHub Pages) may log IP address, browser and pages requested. We don't run our own analytics yet. If we add analytics, we'll update this policy first.

Location (“Near me”)

The “Near me” button is optional. It asks your browser for permission to use your location, and you can decline. If you allow it, your location is used only on your device to calculate distances and sort results. It is never sent to us or stored by us. You can revoke the permission anytime in your browser settings.

Stored on your device

The site uses your browser's localStorage for features that work without an account: favorites, collections, “Been here”, recently viewed, whether you've subscribed, and copies of forms that couldn't be sent. This data stays on your device and isn't sent to us. Clear it anytime from the Saved page or by clearing site data in your browser.

If you install the site or visit it again, a service worker keeps a cache of pages, images and site files on your device so the site loads faster and works offline. The cache holds only public site content, and clearing site data removes it.

How we use it

To review and answer your submissions, run featured placements, send the newsletter you asked for, prevent spam, and meet legal obligations. Hire-a-creator requests may be shared with our video partner, Crenshaw Visuals, so they can send you a quote. Quote requests may be forwarded to the business you asked about.

Service providers & third parties

  • GitHub Pages: hosting.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): our forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, which processes the content you submit.
  • beehiiv: newsletter subscriptions and sending.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers and Leaflet (loaded from unpkg.com): on the Search/map page, your browser requests map images and the map library directly from these third parties, which can see your IP address and the map area requested.
  • Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube: embedded videos (see Cookies).

Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies, embeds & similar technologies

We don't set advertising or tracking cookies ourselves. We use localStorage and a service-worker cache as described above. However, embedded videos from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube may set cookies and collect data when they load or play, under those companies' policies. Map tiles and the Leaflet library are fetched from third-party servers but don't set our cookies. The beehiiv subscribe page and form may use beehiiv's own cookies. You can block third-party cookies in your browser, or open videos on the original platform instead.

Retention

We keep form submissions as long as needed to handle your request and for our business records, generally up to 24 months. We keep newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

Your California privacy rights

If you're a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; delete it; correct it; opt out of sale or sharing (we don't sell or share it); limit use of sensitive personal information (we don't collect any); and not be discriminated against for using these rights. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We'll verify your request by email and respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out request.

Children

The site isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the “last updated” date.
