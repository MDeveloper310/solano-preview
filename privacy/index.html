Privacy Policy

Last updated Oct 30, 2026

⚠️ Template. Have a lawyer review before launch.

Summary

We collect only what you give us through our forms and newsletter, plus basic technical data. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

Information we collect

  • Forms (apply, claim, creator submission, hire-a-creator, advertise, referral): name, email, business details, social handles, reel links and any message you write.
  • Newsletter: your email address and where on the site you signed up, handled by our newsletter provider (beehiiv).
  • Technical data: our host (GitHub Pages) may log IP address, browser and pages requested. We don't run our own analytics yet. If we add analytics, this policy will be updated first.
  • Stored in your browser: small localStorage flags (for example, that you've already subscribed, or a form you saved while offline). These stay on your device.

How we use it

To review and answer your submissions, run featured placements, send the newsletter you asked for, prevent spam, and meet legal obligations. Lead-form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Hire-a-creator requests may be shared with our video partner, Crenshaw Visuals, so they can send you a quote.

Service providers

GitHub (hosting), FormSubmit (form delivery), beehiiv (newsletter), and the video platforms whose embeds appear on the site. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies & embeds

We don't set advertising cookies ourselves. However, embedded videos from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube may set cookies and collect data when you play or load them, under those companies' policies. You can block third-party cookies in your browser settings, or open videos on the original platform instead.

Retention

We keep form submissions as long as needed to handle your request and for our business records, generally up to 24 months. We keep newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

Your California privacy rights

If you're a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; delete it; correct it; opt out of sale or sharing (we don't sell or share it); limit use of sensitive personal information (we don't collect any); and not be discriminated against for using these rights. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. We'll verify your request by email and respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out request.

Children

The site isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the “last updated” date.
