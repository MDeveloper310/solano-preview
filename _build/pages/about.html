About Scroll California

Scroll California is a food video guide to California, organized city by city. Swipe short food videos, tap through to the place for the address, or upload your own.

Who makes it

Scroll California is made by Crenshaw Visuals, a California video studio that shoots cinematic short-form content for restaurants and hotels. Find us on Instagram at @crenshawvisuals.

How we pick spots

  • Videos come from our own studio and from visitors who filmed the food themselves.
  • A person reviews every visitor upload before it goes live. Anyone can report a video.
  • Places are real businesses we or our visitors have filmed. We check every new place before it gets a page.
  • No scraped clips, no stock footage, no invented reviews or star ratings.
  • We're starting small and growing city by city instead of pretending to cover everything.

Sponsors and clients

We make money in two ways: sponsored placements on this site, and the video work our studio does for businesses. To keep that honest:

  • Every paid placement on the site is labeled Sponsored.
  • If our studio has filmed for a business as a paid client, its page says so.
  • If we're ever given a free meal or stay, the page and the video say so.
