More questions

What does claiming cost?

Nothing. Claiming is free forever. Pro is optional: $39/mo, month to month. Founding members get 50% off the first month.

How does pay-per-lead work?

When a customer sends a structured quote request from your page, it lands in your lead inbox. You pay $15 only for real leads; spam and duplicates are free. No monthly minimum.

Can I remove competitor ads from my page?

Yes, with Pro. Unclaimed and free pages show a labeled “Sponsored” row of nearby businesses.

Can I pay to remove a bad review?

No. Reviews can't be bought, removed or reordered by businesses. You can reply publicly and report reviews that break our rules.

Is my data sold?

No. Our analytics are anonymous and first-party. See the Privacy Policy.
