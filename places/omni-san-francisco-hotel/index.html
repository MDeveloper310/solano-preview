Why go

It sits at California and Montgomery, right on the cable car line, so the ride is part of arriving.

The lobby is the photo: a sweeping staircase, crystal chandeliers and dark wood.

In-room dining shows up under silver cloches. In our shoot that meant a burger and fries, a berry dessert and a drink by the window with the downtown towers outside.
