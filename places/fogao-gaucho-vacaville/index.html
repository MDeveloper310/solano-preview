Why go

Gaúcho-style servers carve the meat right at your table, straight off the skewer. That's the whole show, and it's a good one.

Get to the bar early. In our footage the bartenders shake and pour every drink by hand, and the sparkling pours come with a little theater.

The dining room has a floor-to-ceiling wine wall and a spread of sides to build your plate around the meat.

Look for the bronze bull out front.
