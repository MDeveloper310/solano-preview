Hilton Garden Inn Fairfield

Hotel off I-80 with an on-site restaurant and bar serving dinner and drinks.

Why go

The on-site restaurant and bar is open to guests for dinner and drinks.

In our footage: tacos, chips, a cold martini, a dragon-fruit drink, and cocktails out on the patio.

It's off I-80 near Solano Town Center, so it works as a stop between the Bay and Sacramento or wine country.
