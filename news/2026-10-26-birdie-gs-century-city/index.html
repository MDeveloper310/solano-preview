Birdie G's reopens as the flagship restaurant of Fairmont Century Plaza

Summary by Scroll California · Oct 26, 2026 · source dated Oct 26, 2026

Jeremy Fox's Birdie G's, which closed at Santa Monica's Bergamot Station on Dec. 20, 2025, reopened on Monday, Oct. 26 at 2025 Avenue of the Stars in Century City, according to LAeats.

The new location is an all-day California brasserie: coffee from 6 a.m., breakfast and lunch, dinner 5:30–9 p.m., and weekend brunch. LAeats reports the matzo ball soup, koji-aged steaks and rose petal pie are back, and that reservations are on OpenTable.

LAeats also lists Mievè (pizza, La Brea) and Kozo (Afro-Asian, Sunset Strip) as late-October openings to watch.
