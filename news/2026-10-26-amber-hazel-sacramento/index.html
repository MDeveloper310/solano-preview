Amber Hazel Vietnamese Kitchen opens near the Capitol with $19 pho

Summary by Scroll California · Oct 26, 2026 · source dated Oct 26, 2026

Amber Hazel Vietnamese Kitchen opened Oct. 1 at 1714 15th St. in downtown Sacramento, not far from the State Capitol, the Sacramento Bee reported Oct. 26. It replaced Mochinut.

The Bee lists three phos (beef, veggie and chicken) at $19 each, plus banh mi, rice vermicelli and broken rice plates. Drinks include Vietnamese coffee, ube matcha, taro milk and egg coffee, and dessert is che or Gunther's custard ice cream. It's open daily, 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.
