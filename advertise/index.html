What Scroll California is

  • A mobile-first, swipeable video feed of California restaurants, hotel dining and stays.
  • A page for every place, built to be found on Google (video, address, why go) and easy to share.
  • A city guide for every city we cover, and an email list for new spots.
  • Run by Crenshaw Visuals, a studio that shoots cinematic short-form video for restaurants and hotels. The reel here is ours.

Audience: the honest version

Scroll California relaunched in November 2026. We don't have traffic numbers yet, and we won't make any up. That's why founding sponsors get founding prices. As soon as measurement is switched on, every sponsor gets plain counts of how many times their placement was seen and clicked.

What we promise

  • Every paid placement is labeled Sponsored. No disguised ads.
