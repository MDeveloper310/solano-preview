How the Scroll California feed works (and why we never re-upload videos)

By Scroll California · Oct 31, 2026

The Watch feed mixes short videos from TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels about California spots, food, events and news.

How it works:

  • Official players only. Every video plays inside its platform's own embed: YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode), TikTok or Instagram. We never download or re-host anything, and you don't need an account on any of those apps to watch.
  • Creators get credit. Every card shows the creator's handle and platform, with a link to the original post. Views and follows go to the creator.
  • Previews load as you scroll. You see a lightweight preview until you reach a video, and the player only loads then. That keeps the feed fast and stops platforms from loading trackers for videos you never watch.
  • Like counts are ours, not theirs. Hearts and saves count Scroll California activity only. If a video has none yet, we show nothing rather than a fake number.

If you made a video of a California spot, [submit it](../../creators/submit/). If you're a creator and want something removed, use our [takedown page](../../disclosures/).
