About Solano Spotlight

Solano Spotlight is an independent, free directory of local businesses in Vacaville, Fairfield, Suisun City, Vallejo, Benicia, Dixon and Rio Vista.

Listings start from open data (OpenStreetMap, ODbL) and include only factual details. Owners can claim and correct their listing at no cost.

Paid placements are always labeled Featured. Our featured video partner is Crenshaw Visuals, who produces cinematic short-form reels & promo videos for local businesses.
